8th International Workshop on Computer Vision

Stresa, Lake Maggiore, Italy • May 26 – 28, 2026

About the Workshop

The 8th International Workshop on Computer Vision brings together leading minds in AI and vision research. This exclusive event focuses on high-quality invited talks, interactive panels, and the exploration of frontier themes in computer vision.

To foster open dialogue and the sharing of work-in-progress, there are no formal published proceedings. For 2026, we are happy to host the workshop in the historic lakeside town of Stresa, Italy.

The Venue

The workshop meetings will be held at the Hotel La Palma. Set against the backdrop of the Borromean Islands, it offers an inspiring environment for scientific exchange.

Location: Piazzale Europa, 3, 28838 Stresa (VB), Italy.
