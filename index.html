About the Workshop

The 8th International Workshop on Computer Vision brings together leading minds in AI and vision research. This exclusive event focuses on high-quality invited talks, interactive panels, and the exploration of frontier themes in computer vision.

To foster open dialogue and the sharing of work-in-progress, there are no formal published proceedings.

Location

For 2026, we are happy to host the workshop in the historic lakeside town of Stresa, Italy.
